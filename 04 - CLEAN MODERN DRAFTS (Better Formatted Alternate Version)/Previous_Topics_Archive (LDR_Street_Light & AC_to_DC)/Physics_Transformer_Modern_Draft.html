1. AIM & OBJECTIVES

Aim: To design, assemble, and analyze the working of a Step-Down Electrical Transformer, verify the relationship between coil turns and voltages (Vs / Vp = Ns / Np), and calculate its efficiency under resistive load.

Objectives:

  • To study the fundamental principle of Mutual Induction between two coils.
  • To investigate the relationship between input AC voltage (Vp) and output AC voltage (Vs) with respect to turns (Np and Ns).
  • To determine transformation efficiency (η) under load.
  • To understand the four major power losses in transformers and how they are reduced in practice.

2. PRINCIPLE & WORKING THEORY

A transformer works on the principle of Mutual Induction. When alternating current flows through the primary winding, it establishes an alternating magnetic flux in the laminated soft-iron core. This flux links with the secondary coil, inducing an alternating EMF across its terminals according to Faraday’s Law: e = - M • (dI / dt).

TRANSFORMATION RATIO FORMULA:
Vs / Vp = Ns / Np = K
• For a Step-Down Transformer: Ns < Np ⇒ K < 1 and Vs < Vp.
• Conservation of Energy: Vp • Ip = Vs • Is ⇒ Is / Ip = 1 / K (Current steps up: Is > Ip).

Power & Efficiency (Reading 1):

  • Input Power: Pin = Vp • Ip = 220 V × 0.12 A = 26.40 W
  • Output Power: Pout = Vs • Is = 21.2 V × 1.10 A = 23.32 W
  • Efficiency: η = (Pout / Pin) × 100% = (23.32 / 26.40) × 100% = 88.33% (Average practical efficiency: 88.1%).

5. ENERGY LOSSES & MITIGATION

1. Copper Loss (I²R): Heat in windings; minimized by using thick copper wire on secondary.
2. Eddy Current Loss: Induced swirling currents in iron; minimized by using thin, varnished silicon-steel laminations.
3. Hysteresis Loss: Magnetic reversal energy; minimized by using high-permeability soft iron.
4. Flux Leakage: Incomplete coupling; minimized by winding secondary directly over primary.

6. CONCLUSION

The step-down transformer model successfully verified the voltage-to-turns relation (Vs / Vp ≈ Ns / Np). The circuit stepped down 220 V AC to approximately 21.2 V AC with a measured operating efficiency of 88.1% under load.
