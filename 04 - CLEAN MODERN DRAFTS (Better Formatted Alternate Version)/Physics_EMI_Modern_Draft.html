Electromagnetic Induction & Lenz's Law
Experimental Verification of Faraday's Laws using a Solenoid and Bar Magnet
Lokseva E-School, Pune | Class XII - Physics (042) | Candidate: Soham Patne

1. Aim & Theoretical Foundation

To investigate the generation of induced electromotive force (EMF) in an insulated copper wire solenoid by moving a permanent bar magnet along its axis, verifying Faraday's Law and Lenz's Law.

Key Mathematical Formulation:

Magnetic Flux: Φ = B · A · cos(θ)
Faraday-Lenz Law: e = - N · (dΦ / dt)
Induced Current: I = e / R = - (N / R) · (dΦ / dt)

4. Core Inferences & Verification

  • Relative Motion is Essential: Zero deflection is recorded whenever relative speed between magnet and coil is zero, proving that steady flux induces no current.
  • Speed Proportionality: Rapid motion yields more than double the deflection (+26 vs +12 div), confirming e ∝ dΦ/dt.
  • Lenz's Law Confirmation: Deflection reverses polarity upon reversing pole or motion direction. Mechanical work done against magnetic resistance converts to electrical energy.
  • Soft Iron Core Enhancement: High relative permeability (μr > 1000) channels magnetic field lines, driving full-scale needle deflection.
