Comparative Study of Foaming Capacity of Commercial Soaps
Investigating Foam Volume, Persistence & Hard Water Degradation
Lokseva E-School, Pune | Class XII - Chemistry (043) | Candidate: Soham Patne

1. Objective & Saponification Principle

To compare the lathering power of five commercial bathing soaps (Dove, Lux, Santoor, Dettol, Lifebuoy) in distilled water and evaluate the impact of dissolved calcium ions on foam degradation.

Chemical Saponification & Hard Water Reactions:

Saponification: Triglyceride + 3 NaOH → Glycerol + 3 RCOONa (Soap)
Scum Formation: 2 C₁₇H₃₅COO⁻Na⁺ + Ca²⁺ → (C₁₇H₃₅COO)₂Ca ↓ (Sticky Curd) + 2 Na⁺

4. Key Conclusions

  • Foaming Rank Order: Dove > Lux > Santoor > Dettol > Lifebuoy.
  • Total Fatty Matter (TFM): High TFM (>75%) in Grade 1 toilet soaps (Dove, Lux) creates thicker, elastic bubble films that resist drainage and evaporation.
  • Hard Water Scum: Calcium ions precipitate stearate molecules as insoluble curd, cutting foam volume by over 55%.
