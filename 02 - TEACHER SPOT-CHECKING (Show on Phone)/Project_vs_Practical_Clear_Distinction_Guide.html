Feature 1. PRACTICALS (Lab Work & Records) 2. INVESTIGATORY PROJECT (Board Booklet)
What it is Routine CBSE syllabus lab exercises completed across the academic year. A single in-depth, independent research topic chosen from the CBSE approved list.
Document Format Standard school-issued hardbound / spiral Lab Record Notebook. Custom 15-page report booklet, color printed, spiral bound with green borders.
Handwritten vs Printed Mostly Handwritten by student. In IP, SQL tables are handwritten; only Matplotlib chart slips are pasted. 100% Typed & Color Printed (for Physics & Chem). English ASL is transcribed by hand on project paper.
What is ACTUALLY Performed on Exam Day External examiner assigns 2 standard syllabus experiments on the lab spot (e.g., Ohm's law, titration). Student presents their project booklet & model. Answers oral Viva Voce questions based on their topic.
Weightage in Board Exam Lab experiments + Record Notebook = ~18–20 Marks. Investigatory Project + Project Viva = ~7–10 Marks.
SUBJECT-BY-SUBJECT SUMMARY (WHAT SOHAM DOES FOR EACH):
1. PHYSICS: Electromagnetic Induction (Faraday's & Lenz's Law)
• Project Booklet: 15-page color report (Print After Approval).
• What is Performed: On viva day, Soham places the coil on the lab table and moves a magnet inside. The center-zero galvanometer needle deflects, proving current generation. Zero shock hazard.
• Practical File: Regular lab record notebook containing daily experiments done during school hours.
2. CHEMISTRY: Foaming Capacity of Commercial Soaps
• Project Booklet: 15-page color report (Print After Approval).
• What is Performed: NO live chemical boiling or hazardous mixing is done on exam day. The examiner purely asks Viva questions (micelle structure, saponification, scum formation in hard water).
• Practical File: Standard volumetric titration & salt analysis record book.
3. ENGLISH ASL: Smartphone Addiction & Digital Detox Survey
