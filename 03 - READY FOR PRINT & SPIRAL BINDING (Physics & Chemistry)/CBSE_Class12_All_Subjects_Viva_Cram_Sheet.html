1. PHYSICS: STEP-DOWN ELECTRICAL TRANSFORMER
• Working Principle: Mutual Induction (Faraday's Law: e = -M • dI/dt). Alternating current in primary induces alternating voltage in secondary coil.
• Direct Current (DC) Trap: Transformers cannot work on DC because DC is constant (dΦ/dt = 0). Zero induced voltage; pure primary winding will overheat and burn!
• Turns Ratio: K = Vs / Vp = Ns / Np. For Step-Down: Ns < Np ⇒ K < 1, Vs < Vp, and Is > Ip (voltage drops, current rises).
• Why Laminated Core?: To minimize Eddy Current Losses. Thin varnished sheets break circulating currents in the iron block.
• Why Soft Iron Core?: High magnetic permeability and narrow hysteresis loop minimize heating during 50 Hz magnetization reversals.
• 4 Major Losses: (1) Copper loss (I²R), (2) Eddy current loss, (3) Hysteresis loss, (4) Flux leakage.
2. CHEMISTRY: STUDY OF COMMON FOOD ADULTERANTS
• Starch in Milk / Butter: Test: Boil sample, cool, add 2-3 drops of Iodine solution. Observation: Intense deep blue-black colour confirms starch.
• Chalk in Sugar: Test: Dissolve sugar in water, filter residue, add Dilute HCl. Observation: Brisk effervescence of CO₂ gas turning lime water milky confirms chalk (CaCO₃).
• Metanil Yellow in Turmeric: Test: Shake turmeric in water, add Concentrated HCl. Observation: Persistent magenta-pink colour that does not fade on dilution with water confirms toxic Metanil Yellow dye.
• Lead Chromate in Chilli: Test: Digest chilli powder in Conc. HNO₃, filter, add 10% KI solution. Observation: Bright yellow precipitate of Lead Iodide (PbI₂) confirms toxic lead chromate.
• FSSAI / Safety Tips: Always buy certified sealed packages with FSSAI/AGMARK logos; avoid loose unbranded market spices.
