📐 MOM'S MECHANICAL TABLE-RULING GUIDE
Step-by-Step Instructions to Rule SQL Output Tables with Ruler & Pencil
Lokseva E-School | Class 12 IP (065) Practical File | Candidate: Soham Patne
🌟 THE 4 GOLDEN STEPS FOR MOM:
  • Step 1 (Soham Writes First): Let Soham write the column headers and numbers/text in his normal handwriting on the plain white left page.
  • Step 2 (Outer Rectangle): Place your 30cm ruler horizontally and vertically to draw a neat outer box enclosing Soham's written text (leave about 2–3 mm space around his letters).
  • Step 3 (Header Divider Line): Draw a crisp horizontal line right underneath the top column names (e.g., under student_id | name | marks).
  • Step 4 (Column Separators): Draw straight vertical lines between the columns to turn the text into an authentic database table.
